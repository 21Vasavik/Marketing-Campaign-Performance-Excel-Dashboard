# 📊 Marketing Campaign Performance Excel Dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze marketing campaign performance across channels, regions, and time periods. The project focuses on profitability, efficiency, ROAS, CTR, CPC, and conversions to support data-driven marketing decisions.

## 🎯 Business Problem

Marketing teams need to understand which channels and regions are delivering the best results and whether advertising spend is generating sufficient revenue.

This dashboard helps answer:

- Which marketing channel has the highest ROAS?
- How are spend and revenue changing over time?
- Which region generates the most conversions?
- How is the marketing budget distributed across channels?
- How efficiently are impressions converted into clicks and conversions?
- Which channels provide better profitability?

## 📐 Key Metrics

- **CTR** = Clicks / Impressions
- **CPC** = Spend / Clicks
- **CPA** = Spend / Conversions
- **ROAS** = Revenue / Spend
- **Profit** = Revenue - Spend
- **Profit Margin %** = Profit / Revenue

## 📊 Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes:

- 💰 Total Revenue

- 📈 Total Profit

- 📊 ROAS
- 🖱️ CTR
- 💲 CPC

- 🌍 Region-wise Conversions

## 💡 Key Insights

- Total revenue generated was approximately **$6.55M**.
- Total marketing spend was approximately **$3.13M**.
- The campaigns generated approximately **$3.42M profit**.
- Overall ROAS was approximately **2.09**, meaning every $1 spent generated about $2.09 in revenue.
- **Instagram** recorded the highest ROAS among the channels at approximately **2.29**.
- **West** generated the highest number of conversions with approximately **48.7K**.
- The funnel shows a significant drop from impressions to clicks and from clicks to conversions, highlighting opportunities to improve campaign engagement and conversion efficiency.

## 🚀 Recommendations

- Prioritize high-performing channels such as Instagram and Facebook.
- Review LinkedIn and Twitter campaigns to improve efficiency and ROAS.
- Investigate the conversion funnel to improve click-through and conversion rates.
- Optimize budget allocation based on channel profitability and ROAS.
- Continuously monitor monthly spend and revenue trends.
